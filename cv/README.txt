This folder is for your CV.

No CV has been added yet. Until you add one, the "Download CV" buttons
on the website show a friendly "coming soon" message instead.

To turn on CV downloads:

1. Save your CV as a PDF.
2. Name it exactly:  Ahmad-CV.pdf
3. Put it in this "cv" folder.
4. Open script.js, find SITE_SETTINGS at the top, and change
       cvAvailable: false,
   to
       cvAvailable: true,
5. Save script.js and refresh the page in your browser.
