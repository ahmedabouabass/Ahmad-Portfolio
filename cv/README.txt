This folder is for your CV.

Your CV is here as Ahmad-CV.pdf, and the "Download CV" buttons on the
website download it.

To update your CV later:

1. Save the new version as a PDF.
2. Name it exactly:  Ahmad-CV.pdf
3. Put it in this "cv" folder, replacing the old file.
4. Refresh the page in your browser.

To turn CV downloads off (the buttons then show a "coming soon" message):
open script.js, find SITE_SETTINGS at the top, and change
    cvAvailable: true,
to
    cvAvailable: false,
