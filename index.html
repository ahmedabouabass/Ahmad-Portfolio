01 — About

About Me

I am a primary educator with over ten years of classroom experience in Riyadh, across the Cambridge, British, IB and American curricula, and I specialise in supporting learners who use English as an Additional Language (EAL).

My training in Teaching English as a Second Language, together with my Teaching Diploma, shapes how I plan lessons that are clear, structured and accessible to every learner. As a certified trainer (Training of Trainers, University of London), I also enjoy sharing practice and supporting colleagues.

I am currently studying for an MSc in Psychology of Mental Health and Wellbeing, which deepens my understanding of how learners think, feel and grow — inside and outside the classroom.
